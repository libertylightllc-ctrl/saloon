How It Works

The app never forces VAT or invoices on a non-VAT barber shop

VAT Off Sale is saved as an internal business record. No VAT, no TRN and no tax invoice fields are required.
Simple Receipt Optional customer receipt with salon name, service, amount, staff and payment method.
VAT On For registered salons only. TRN, VAT amount, invoice numbering and tax reports become active.
