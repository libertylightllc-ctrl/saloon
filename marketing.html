01 — The maths nobody shows you

A forty-dirham haircut costs you five percent.

Not two and a half. The fixed fee is what does it — and at a barbershop ticket, the fixed fee is most of the bill. Here is one real cut, priced honestly.

The UAE central bank caps domestic debit interchange at 0.75% card-present. You are not paying interchange — you are paying acquirer margin plus a flat fee that never shrinks with your ticket. On an AED 30 cut it is 5.8%.

02 — Three things no salon platform has built

Everyone else sells you a calendar.

Fresha, Zenoti, Salonist, Booksy — every one is a booking engine with a payment take-rate attached. Your problem was never an empty calendar. It is not knowing where the cash and the stock went.

GAP 01

Stock that empties itself

Put the recipe on the service — blade one, foam 8ml, developer 20ml — and the shelf empties as you sell. Cost of goods per cut, margin per service, and an alert the day blade consumption outruns shave count.

That last one catches theft and satisfies the municipality's single-use-tool rule in the same query.

Only one enterprise product auto-deducts back-bar stock, at $300–600 per location plus implementation.
GAP 02

The day, actually closed

Expected against counted. A reason required for the variance before it can be approved, the approver's name on it, and the whole thing written to a log that later edits cannot rewrite.

Cash tips captured and split — evenly, by hours, by points, or as a percentage tip-out — with a screen each barber can open to see their own arithmetic.

Not one platform reviewed markets cash-drawer reconciliation or cash-tip pooling. The category-leading POS cannot pool cash tips at all.
GAP 03

The inspector's file

Dubai Municipality asks for cleaning logs, sterilizer cycles, quarterly water tests, monthly tank checks, pest control certificates and a valid health card for every barber — and its own guideline permits an electronic system in place of paper.

One screen holds all of it, computes what is overdue from the calendar, and prints a pack you can hand over.

No salon platform in the world models UAE municipal compliance. It has no revenue outside one country — which is exactly why it stays open.

03 — On the device the shop actually uses

A price board, not a spreadsheet.

The barber holds a phone, standing, one hand, in a bright room. So the service is a tile, the price is large, the target is 56 pixels, and the charge button never leaves the thumb.

01

Four taps to a recorded sale

Category, service, payment, charge. Staff, time, discount reason and stock movement are captured without anyone typing them.

02

Arabic that is the same product

Not a mirrored afterthought. Same weights, same rhythm, Eastern Arabic numerals, drawn in a typeface with a real Arabic cut. Hindi and Urdu on the same footing.

03

Keeps selling when the wifi drops

Sales and shifts work offline and sync when you reconnect. Refunds and drawer counts wait for a connection, on purpose — those are decisions, not entries.

04

A receipt on WhatsApp, usually free

When the customer messages first, the reply window is free. We build the flow that way, so your receipts and reminders cost almost nothing.

05

The owner sees it from home

Every branch, every barber, every day. Not one browser on one tablet behind the till.
